Klingon Postal Course

Created by Dave Barron and intended as a supplement to The Klingon Dictionary, the Postal Course introduces students to the vocabulary and grammar of tlhIngan Hol (Klingon language), building comprehension and familiarity as they progress through an eleven-lesson series. To enroll, send a self-addressed, stamped (adequate for two ounces) legal-size envelope to:

Klingon Language Lessons
c/o Eric Andeen
2323 North Central Avenue, 1801
Phoenix, AZ 85004 USA

You can also download the lessons in PDF form (you will need a PDF reader in order to view these files). You can submit your answers via e-mail, thereby avoiding postage--particularly useful if you live outside of North America!
