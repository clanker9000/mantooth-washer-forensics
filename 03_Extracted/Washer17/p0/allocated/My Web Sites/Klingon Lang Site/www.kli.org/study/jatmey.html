jatmey

The Literary Supplement of the KLI

Scholarly study of a language is more than just nouns and verbs (and chuvmey). Language is a medium for art as well as communication. In recognition of this, the KLI publishes jatmey (roughly, "Scattered Tongues") approximately annually, as a vehicle for original poetry and fiction composed entirely in the Klingon Language. Issues of jatmey are available for order from the KLI.
