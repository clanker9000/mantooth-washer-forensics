HolQeD Online

The KLI's scholarly journal, HolQeD, has changed to electronic-only distribution. KLI members can download PDF files of the journal issues.

Be sure you have logged in as a KLI member so that you can download the journal. You will need Adobe Acrobat Reader or another suitable PDF-reader to read these files.
