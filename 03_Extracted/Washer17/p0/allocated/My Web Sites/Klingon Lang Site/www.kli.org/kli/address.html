Contacting the KLI

For more information about any of the Klingon Language Institute's activities, contact its director, Dr. Lawrence Schoen (lawrence@kli.org, or at the address below).

To join the KLI, or order back-issues of HolQeD or any of the products offered by the KLI, you can order using a credit card or simply send a check in US currency payable to the Klingon Language Institute to:

Klingon Language Institute
P.O. Box 634
Flourtown, PA 19031-0634
USA

Annual individual membership in the KLI (including a four-issue subscription to HolQeD) is US$15 for people inside the United States, US$18 in Canada, and US$21 for overseas subscribers. Annual institutional membership is US$24 in the US, US$27 in Canada, and US$30 overseas.
