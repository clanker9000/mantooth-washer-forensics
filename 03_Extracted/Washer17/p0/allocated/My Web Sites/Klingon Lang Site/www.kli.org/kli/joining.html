How to Join the Klingon Language Institute

Joining the KLI is simple; it is open to all individuals interested in the study of the Klingon language. General membership in the KLI along with a year's subscription to HolQeD costs $16 in the USA, $20 in Canada and Mexico, and $25 for all other countries (payment in U.S. funds only). Institutional subscriptions are slightly higher. All members receive a free copy of the annual KLI Directory.

You can join the KLI via our website using your VISA or MasterCard. If you don't have a credit card you can simply send a cheque or money order (made payable to KLI) to:

Klingon Language Institute
P.O. Box 634
Flourtown, PA 19031 USA

If you're already a member (or might still be), you can check your membership status and find out just when your membership expires (or expired). Currently, this feature is available only for our members in the United States.
