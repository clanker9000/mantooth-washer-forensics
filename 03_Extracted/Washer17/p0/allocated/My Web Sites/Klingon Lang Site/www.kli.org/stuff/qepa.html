qep'a' wa'maH loSDIch

July 25-29, 2007

The fourteenth qep'a' has been scheduled, by dint of great effort against short time, to begin in the evening of Wednesday July 25, 2007, and run until Sunday July 29th. Wednesday is unofficial and informal (there is no formal opening-night banquet, as we have sometimes had).

The location is as it was last year:

Comfort Inn Philadelphia Airport
53 Industrial Hwy
Essington, PA, US 19029
Phone: (610) 532-9800

Room charges are $85/night (plus applicable taxes), which includes "continental breakfast." The qep'a' itself is expected to run approximately $30 per person (please pay at the event, due to problems with the KLI Merchant Page. Bring cash if possible).

When calling to make your reservation, make sure to specify that you are with the KLI conference.

Deadline for our room block is June 30th, which doesn't leave a lot of time—but then, we were running short of time to start with.
