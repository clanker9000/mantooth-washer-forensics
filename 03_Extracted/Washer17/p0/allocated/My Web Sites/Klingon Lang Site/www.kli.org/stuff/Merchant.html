Products Available from
the Klingon Language Institute

We are pleased to be able to make the following products available through this WWW interface. Some of these products are exclusive to the KLI, some are published by Simon & Schuster, the publishing company used by Paramount. Be advised that incoming orders are generally processed in batches, usually every two weeks, and your credit card will not be charged until your order is ready to be shipped.

Ordering merchandise from this page is a two-step process: first you must identify your "postal zone" and only then can you select the items you wish to purchase.

In addition to the costs listed with each item below, you will see a range of shipping and handling fees. These charges vary with your location and reflect our costs in getting your order to you. As such, it is critical that you select the correct "zone" below before you proceed any further. All orders (with the exception of membership/renewal and qep'a' registration) begin with a base handling charge of two dollars. Additional shipping charges are added as a function of your location and the merchandise ordered. Specifically, the total weight of your order is computed, and rounded up to the next whole ounce. Then, an "allowance," which depends on the shipping zone, is deducted, and a shipping cost is assessed on the remaining weight by the ounce.
