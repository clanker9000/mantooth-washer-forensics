Send an Electronic Greeting Card with the KLI

Welcome to the KLI's eCard section. You can use this free service to send email greeting cards to your friends and family. Select a card from this page. Then you will be able to fill in the name and email address of the sender (you) and the recipient, as well as a personalized message from you. The recipient will receive email containing a link to a page at this site which will show your card.

We would appreciate any feedback you may have on this service and its functioning, including bug reports, compliments, etc.
