Funny Pics of Oppossums singing in a Napier Tourist Shop in Hawkes Bay

Funny Pics of dead Oppossums singing in a Tourist Shop in Napier on the North Island of New Zealand. It is a must see for a New Zealand Tourist or Traveller. You will find more Funny Pics there! The possum was introduced to New Zealand in 1837 to establish a fur trade. However, the extremely favourable conditions in New Zealand led to an explosion of possum populations which now threatens native vegetation and trees. This Australian import often bares the brunt of kiwi humour in TV ads or in displays like this - however they do make funny pics.
